Colophons may be in Sanskrit, Newari (Nevārī), Nepali or Maithili, or a mix.

Identify every HUMAN person named in the colophon portion that concerns the making of this copy: the scribe, whoever commissioned or paid for the copy, donors, owners, the beneficiary of merit, the reigning king or queen, ministers, teachers, and relatives named to identify them (father, grandfather, sons, wives, brothers). Names: give "name" in IAST stem form (e.g. ānandakareṇa → Ānandakara; lakṣmīpatīnām → Lakṣmīpati; jayasiṃhadevasya → Jayasiṃhadeva for kings keep -deva / -malla as part of the regnal name). Date: copy the colophon's own date; era as written (Newari colophons with "saṃvat" and a three-digit year in Nepal are normally NS; "Lakṣmaṇasena"/"la saṃ" is LS). ce_approx: NS+880, VS−57, ŚS+78, LS+1119; leave empty if unsure.
