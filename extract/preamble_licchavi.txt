NOTE ON THIS RECORD: it is not a manuscript colophon but a Licchavi-period inscription from the Kathmandu Valley (5th–9th century), in an edition that gives an English header (place, description, the editor's remarks, and a concordance: Gn. = Gnoli, DV = Dhanavajra Vajracarya, HJ, R. = Regmi), the TEXT in IAST with line numbers (lacunae as dots or dashes, restorations in [ ] and ( )), and NOTES. Treat the whole inscription as the "colophon" below, and the editor's header and notes as help in reading it.

Adapt the instructions that follow to an inscription:
- Persons: every HUMAN person the inscription names: the king or feudatory issuing it (e.g. "śrīmahāsāmantāṃśuvarmā kuśalī ... samājñāpayati"), the dūtaka who carried out the order ("dūtako 'tra yuvarājaḥ ..."), officials and offices (sarvadaṇḍanāyaka, mahāpratīhāra, sāmanta, kumārāmātya, ...), donors and founders of images, temples, water conduits or land grants, recipients of grants (persons, not deities or institutions), the composer of a praśasti, the engraver or writer (utkīrṇa, likhita), teachers, and kin named to identify any of them. Earlier kings named as ancestors are role "relative_only" unless they act in the inscription.
- Roles: use "king" for the reigning or issuing ruler (also a mahāsāmanta issuing an edict in his own name), "dutaka" for the dūtaka, "official" for other officers named with an office, "donor" for founders and donors, "recipient" for persons receiving a grant, "composer" for the author of the verses, "engraver" for the engraver or writer of the inscription; "commissioner" when someone had the work made. Put titles and offices (mahārāja, bhaṭṭāraka, mahāsāmanta, yuvarāja, sarvadaṇḍanāyaka, gomin, bhikṣu, ...) in "titles".
- Names: keep -varman, -deva, -gupta, -gomin as part of the name (Aṃśuvarman, Bhogavarman, Jiṣṇugupta, Śivadeva); remove śrī. Do not list deities (Paśupati, Nārāyaṇa ...), vihāras, villages or guṭhis as persons; villages go in "place".
- Date: copy the date as written (saṃvat, month, pakṣa, tithi, nakṣatra, muhūrta, weekday). Give "era" as "unspecified" and "ce_approx" empty: the date is converted later. In "place", give the find-spot or the place named as the site of the foundation.
- "purpose": what the inscription records (land grant, edict, foundation of an image, water conduit, ...).
